ⓘ A note on data: There are an estimated 915 community lands across Kenya. The 105 on this dashboard are the ones DLCI currently holds information on. Of these 105, 79 are recorded as fully registered under the Community Land Act 2016, including the 64 listed in the Ministry of Lands registration status report of 29 September 2025 — but we currently hold confirmed location and detailed profile information for only 58 of them; the rest are pending fuller documentation. Coverage varies by community — some records (boundaries, population, registration dates) are still pending confirmation and will be updated as more accurate information becomes available.
Where communities stand
By County

The 105 communities DLCI has documented, broken down by county — click one to see the individual communities. Each is at a different stage: some have secured a full title, some are still in the process of registration, and others have only had an inventory submitted with no formal process yet started. Most trace back to former group ranches that converted their land tenure; a smaller number are historical, un-adjudicated former trust lands.
